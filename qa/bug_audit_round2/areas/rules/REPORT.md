It omits FEN fullmove and serialized repetition history from reflected-state equality and starts each comparison with an empty occurrence map; it does not claim to independently prove history-dependent draw semantics. The TS probe's `castles` diagnostic only counts king-home UCI signatures and its EP counter was not instrumented; neither is used as actual special-move coverage. Actual special flags come from the separate Dart probe.

The Dart probe separately checks exact turn/fullmove and halfmove accounting, capture material count, one king of each color, no resurrected castling rights, immutable retained positions and king safety. Starting positions include orthodox opening, both-side castling rights, promotion, pinned EP and legal EP near a draw counter. Five positions and20 seed walks are not20 independent starting FENs.
